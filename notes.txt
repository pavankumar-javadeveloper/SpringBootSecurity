Section 20:(How to disable formLogin and httpBasicAuthentication)
===========
-> when we enable the form login( http.formLogin(withDefaults());) UsernamePasswordAuthenticationFilter will takes care the
   extracting the username name password.
   if we disable  http.formLogin -> UsernamePasswordAuthenticationFilter will not extract username&pwd.
-> when we are using basic type of login, BasicAuthenticationFilter will take care the extracting the username and password.
